# Forehead EMG electrode flex, Rev A draft

A single 2-layer polyimide flex that carries seven skin electrodes across the
forehead and temple and runs back above the right ear to the main board's FPC
connector (J6, Hirose FH34SRJ-12S-0.5SH) at the back of the head.

![layout](docs/flex_layout.png)

Status: draft for discussion. DRC is clean in KiCad 7 (0 violations, 0
unconnected, zones filled); the board has not been fitted on a head yet.

## Files

Everything is generated by `gen_flex.py` (`pip install shapely matplotlib`):
edit the constants at the top (electrode sites, tail side and length, pin
order, ear keep-out) and re-run. Don't edit the KiCad files by hand; changes
would be lost on the next run.

- `kicad/emg_flex_electrodes.kicad_pro`: the KiCad project. Open this.
- `kicad/emg_flex_electrodes.kicad_sch`: schematic, 7 electrodes and the
  12-contact tail, connected by net labels. Its symbol UUIDs match the board's
  footprints, so "Update PCB from Schematic" finds no changes.
- `kicad/emg_flex_electrodes.kicad_pcb`: the routed board. Zones are saved
  unfilled: press **B** after opening.
- `kicad/emg_flex.kicad_sym`, `kicad/emg_flex.pretty/`: project symbol and
  footprint libraries (electrode domes 8 and 12 mm, FPC tail), wired up through
  the project's `sym-lib-table` and `fp-lib-table`.
- `kicad/emg_flex_electrodes.kicad_dru`: lets the FPC contacts sit 0.2 mm from
  the tab edge.
- `docs/flex_layout.png`: drawing of the outline, electrodes, routing and ear.

Checks (KiCad 7 command-line and Python API): DRC clean with zones filled (0
violations, 0 unconnected); the schematic netlist matches every board pad's
net and footprint. Files are written in KiCad 7 format and open in KiCad 9/10,
which upgrade them on save.

## Ear clearance

The ear is modelled as an ellipse 120 to 184 mm along the band from the
forehead midline, with the top of the helix 8 mm above the brow line (it is
usually at or below brow level, so this is conservative). The generator
refuses to write a board that comes within 12 mm of it; the current layout
clears it by 21 mm. The crossover block steps up to 26 mm above the brow
before the ear, and the tail runs 30 to 38 mm above the brow over it, which
also keeps it clear of glasses arms. The ear outline and keep-out are drawn on
the board's `User.Drawings` layer.

## Electrode sites

Coordinates are arc length along the band from the forehead midline (+ toward
the tail, which runs along the subject's right) and height above the eyebrow
line. The tail side is `TAIL_SIDE` in `gen_flex.py`; it follows the main
board's connector (see below).

| Electrode | Net | Site (x, y) mm | Muscle and gesture | Pair axis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E1 / E2 | CH1 +/- | (30, 34) / (30, 14) | Right frontalis, above mid-pupil: brow raise | Vertical, along the fibres, 20 mm |
| E3 / E4 | CH2 +/- | (-10, 8) / (-25, 8) | Left corrugator, just above the inner brow: frown | Horizontal, along the fibres, 15 mm |
| E5 / E6 | CH3 +/- | (85, 34) / (85, 14) | Right anterior temporalis at the temple: jaw clench | Vertical, along the anterior fibres, 20 mm |
| E7 | REF / RLD | (0, 37) | Upper midline forehead, between the frontalis bellies | Ø12 mm for lower contact impedance |

Why these three:

- They give three gestures a player can make independently while playing:
  raise brows, frown, clench. Brow raise is the signal used in the earlier
  experiments (`signal_acquisition/data_2025-02-28_raisebrows.csv`).
- All three sit on skin a headband covers and that is usually free of hair.
  The temporalis site is at the front of the temple; on people with a low
  hairline it may sit on hair and need repositioning.
- Temporalis is how a headband gets "upper jaw" activity. The masseter is
  below the zygomatic arch and out of reach of a band; clench shows up
  strongly on anterior temporalis anyway.
- One side per muscle is enough because these gestures are bilateral. The
  corrugator pair is on the left and frontalis on the right to put distance
  between them. Expect crosstalk anyway: corrugator and the lower frontalis
  overlap at the brow, so firmware should treat CH1/CH2 as a mixed pair.
- REF at the upper midline sits over the thin aponeurosis between the two
  frontalis bellies, the quietest skin on the forehead.

Spacing follows SENIAM: 20 mm between electrode centres, along the fibre
direction. Corrugator is a small muscle, so its pair is at 15 mm. Pads are
Ø8 mm.

## Construction

- 2-layer polyimide, 0.11 mm, ENIG, yellow or black coverlay (JLCPCB flex).
  The outline is 298 × 43 mm, inside JLC's 234 × 490 mm flex limit.
- Skin side is **B**. Electrodes are Ø8 mm (REF Ø12) ENIG pads exposed on B,
  with a 2.7 mm plated hole for an optional domed rivet soldered on the outer
  (F) side.
- Signals run on **F** (outer). **B** carries a hatched AGND shield between
  the traces and the skin, so traces do not pick up the skin they cross and
  the band stays flexible. It is tied to the three AGND pins.
- Along the tail the 12 conductors keep the connector order at 0.5 mm pitch,
  so each P/N pair stays adjacent (small loop area) with an AGND guard between
  channels. A crossover block in front of the ear re-orders them with short
  B.Cu jumps so every electrode can leave the bundle without crossings.
- The three REF pins are joined into one REF net at the crossover.
- Stiffener (drawn on the `Stiffener` user layer, exported as
  `fab/*-Stiffener.gbr`): 0.2 mm PI on the B side of the FPC tab, giving
  about 0.31 mm with the 0.11 mm flex (Hirose asks for 0.30 ± 0.03 mm). Rev 1
  has no stiffeners at the electrodes; the foam disc behind each lobe supports
  the rivet. Add FR4 rings there in rev 2 if pads start to lift.

## Electrodes

Rev 1 needs no custom parts. Each site is an ENIG pad (Ø8 mm, REF Ø12 mm)
with a 2.7 mm plated hole, and works two ways:

1. **Flat pad (default, nothing to buy).** The gold pad is the electrode.
   On the hairless forehead this is enough electrically; what matters is
   steady pressure, so put a 2 to 3 mm closed-cell foam disc behind each
   electrode lobe (between flex and band). Many forehead headbands use flat
   contacts this way.
2. **Domed rivet (Liam's pick for rev 1).** A standard DIN 660 / ISO 1051
   round-head solid rivet, 2.5 × 3 mm, in copper: head Ø4.4 mm, 1.5 mm
   tall, a ready-made dome. Push it through the hole from the skin side, trim
   the shank flush to about 1 mm and solder it to the pad on the outer side
   (a hot iron; the rivet sinks heat). Copper solders easily and is skin-safe;
   it tarnishes, so wipe it before use or tin the head. The 3 mm size
   (Ø5.2 × 1.8 mm head) needs `DRILL = 3.1` in `gen_flex.py`.

Other stock options, not recommended for rev 1:

- SMD pogo pins (e.g. YIYUAN YTC1P-2010-01, LCSC C5221287, 2 mm, 3 mm tall):
  push through hair but have a small, pointed contact.
- A male ECG snap stud, to use stick-on gel electrodes when validating sites.

Bar ("wedge") electrodes like Delsys's are not sold as parts; they would be
custom. A custom silver dome (Ø6 mm, 1.2 mm tall, Ø1.4 mm shank) remains the
rev 2 upgrade if the rivets work but feel too sharp.

Dry contacts improve once the skin is a little moist; expect higher impedance
and more motion artefact in the first minutes.

## Ordering at JLCPCB

Upload `kicad/fab/emg_flex_electrodes_gerbers.zip` (gerbers, Excellon drill,
stiffener layer) and choose:

| Option | Value |
|---|---|
| Base material | Flex |
| Layers | 2 |
| Size | 303 × 43 mm (detected from the outline) |
| Thickness | 0.11 mm |
| Copper | 1/3 oz (0.25 mm tracks and gaps) |
| Coverlay | Yellow |
| Surface finish | ENIG, 1 µ" |
| Stiffener | Polyimide, 0.2 mm, one area: the FPC tab on the back (B) side, as drawn in the Stiffener gerber |
| Assembly | None (rivets and foam are fitted by hand) |

The board has 7 electrode pads with 2.7 mm plated holes, 0.3 mm vias and
0.25 mm tracks, all within JLC's regular flex rules. Before ordering, check the
tab against the Hirose FPC drawing and the main board's J6 orientation (below).

Rivets: DIN 660 round head, copper, 2.5 × 3 mm (head Ø4.4 × 1.5 mm), one per
electrode, 7 per board.

## Mating with the main board

The tail carries the main board's J6 pin order (hardware/emg-rev-a-kicad,
commit c72bb90): 1 AGND, 2/3 CH1 P/N, 4 REF, 5 AGND, 6/7 CH2 P/N, 8 REF, 9
AGND, 10/11 CH3 P/N, 12 REF. The tab is 6.5 mm wide with 0.3 × 2.5 mm contacts
at 0.5 mm pitch. The crossover block re-orders lanes, so a later pin order
change on the main board only means editing `PINS` and regenerating.

J6 sits on the main board's F side at its right edge, rotated so the FPC
enters from the right (mounting-tab side) with pin 1 toward the top of the
board. Assuming the main board is worn with F facing out and its USB-C
pointing down, that opening faces the subject's right, so the tail runs along
the right side. Seen from outside, the tail arrives with its outer side (F) up
and pin 1 at the top edge, which lands on J6 pin 1. The FH34SRJ makes contact
on either face of the FPC, so contacts on F work.

Check before ordering:

- **Main board orientation on the head.** If it is worn the other way up or
  with F facing the head, the tail must come from the other side or the pin
  order mirrors: set `TAIL_SIDE` or reverse `PINS` and regenerate.
- **Tail length.** `X_END` puts the tab end 270 mm along the band from the
  midline: half of Liam's 58 cm head (290 mm), minus about 27 mm from the back
  centre to J6's contacts on the 62.5 mm main board (c72bb90), plus about 7 mm
  slack.
  Recheck if the main board layout moves J6.
- **Elastic bands.** A flex does not stretch. If the band is elastic, fix the
  flex at the forehead and at the connector and leave slack (or a sewn S-loop)
  along the tail.
- **Tab and contacts** against the Hirose FH34SRJ FPC drawing (width G = 6.5
  ± 0.05 mm for 12 positions, thickness 0.30 ± 0.03 mm).
